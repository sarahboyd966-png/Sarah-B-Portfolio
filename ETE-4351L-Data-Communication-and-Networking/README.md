# ETE 4351L - Data Communication and Networking

Selected laboratory reports covering signal analysis, sampling, modulation, digital communications, and network protocols. Reports are organized newest-to-oldest so the most advanced work appears first.

## Laboratory Reports

### Lab 12 - Analyzing TCP/UDP Network Traffic
Used Wireshark and SocketTest to generate and inspect TCP and UDP traffic, verify the TCP three-way handshake, and analyze encapsulation across Ethernet, IPv4, and transport-layer protocols.

### Lab 11 - UART
Examined UART communication using a USB-to-serial interface, including baud rate, start/data/stop bits, ASCII data, and oscilloscope measurements of transmitted serial frames.

### Lab 10 - Quadrature Amplitude Modulation
Used MATLAB Communications Toolbox to analyze ASK, PSK, and QAM constellation diagrams and investigate how SNR, transmit power, and modulation choice affect bit and symbol error performance.

### Lab 9 - Line Codes
Implemented and compared Manchester, NRZ, unipolar RZ, and bipolar RZ encoding schemes while examining bandwidth, DC content, and synchronization considerations.

### Lab 8 - Amplitude Shift Keying
Built and analyzed ASK modulation and demodulation using a pseudo-random data generator and analog multiplexer, with emphasis on carrier switching, bandwidth, and frequency content.

### Labs 6-7 - PSK Modulation and Demodulation
Created a pseudo-random data generator with an LFSR and implemented BPSK modulation and demodulation in Multisim and on a breadboard, including phase detection and signal recovery.

### Lab 5 - Sampling and Pulse Amplitude Modulation
Built and analyzed a sampling circuit to study pulse amplitude modulation, the Nyquist sampling criterion, aliasing, and practical sample-and-hold behavior.

### Lab 4 - PWM and Demodulation
Generated pulse-width modulation by comparing a message sine wave with a higher-frequency triangle wave, then recovered the analog message using low-pass filtering and frequency-domain analysis.

### Lab 3 - Sampling Simulation
Used PSpice to simulate signal sampling at multiple sampling frequencies, examine time- and frequency-domain behavior, and investigate sample-and-hold operation.

### Lab 2 - Review of Spectrums
Compared sine, pulse, and triangular-wave spectra using a physical spectrum analyzer, PSpice simulation, and MATLAB Fourier-series calculations.

### Lab 1 - Differential Signaling
Used PSpice op-amp circuits to add common-mode noise to a signal and demonstrate how differential signaling and a difference amplifier can reject that noise.

## Tools and Methods

MATLAB, MATLAB Communications Toolbox, PSpice, Multisim, Wireshark, SocketTest, oscilloscopes, spectrum analyzers, waveform generators, breadboard prototyping, Fourier analysis, digital modulation, and network protocol analysis.
