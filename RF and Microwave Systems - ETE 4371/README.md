# RF and Microwave Systems — ETE 4371

Laboratory reports and technical demonstrations covering transmission lines, impedance matching, RF measurement, antennas, filters, amplifiers, and microwave propagation. Reports are organized newest-to-oldest so the most advanced RF and microwave work appears first.

## Laboratory Reports and Demonstrations

### [Waveguide Demonstration](./01%20-%20Waveguide%20Demonstration.pdf)
Examined a 10 GHz microwave waveguide measurement system and the operation of waveguide-to-coax adapters, fixed and variable attenuators, resonant-cavity frequency meters, slotted lines, directional couplers, terminations, and horn antennas. Connected standing waves, wavelength, power attenuation, reflections, and radar measurement concepts to practical microwave hardware.

### [Impedance Matching Demonstration](./02%20-%20Impedance%20Matching%20Demonstration.pdf)
Investigated RF impedance matching with quarter-wave transformers and single-stub transmission-line matching. Used Smith-chart concepts, SOLT calibration, adjustable transmission lines, and impedance/admittance transformations to show how mismatched loads can be transformed toward a 50 Ω match, with applications to microstrip and PCB design.

### [Lab 10 — RF Filter Design and Amplifiers](./03%20-%20Lab%2010%20-%20RF%20Filter%20Design%20and%20Amplifiers.pdf)
Designed matched 50 Ω RF filters using normalized prototype tables and frequency scaling, comparing Butterworth and Chebyshev responses and their roll-off/ripple tradeoffs. Also analyzed an RF amplifier using VNA S-parameters, forward gain, input/output matching, VSWR, and attenuation practices for safe measurement.

### [Lab 9 — Antennas and S-Parameters](./04%20-%20Lab%209%20-%20Antennas%20and%20S-Parameters.pdf)
Performed two-port SOLT calibration and used S11, S12, S21, and S22 to characterize filters and attenuators with a vector network analyzer. Extended the analysis to antennas using Smith charts, log-magnitude return loss, SWR, resonance, and construction/testing of a quarter-wave antenna.

### [Lab 8 — Slotted Line and VSWR Measurements](./05%20-%20Lab%208%20-%20Slotted%20Line%20and%20VSWR%20Measurements.pdf)
Measured standing-wave voltage along a slotted transmission line for matched, open, short, 25 Ω, and 200 Ω terminations. Identified peaks and nulls, normalized spatial voltage data, and related the measured patterns to VSWR, reflection coefficient, impedance mismatch, and standing-wave behavior.

### [Lab 7B — LC Transmission Line Measurements](./06%20-%20Lab%207B%20-%20LC%20Transmission%20Line%20Measurements.pdf)
Studied distributed transmission-line behavior with an 18-section LC network using the RLGC model, characteristic impedance, propagation constant, and VSWR concepts. Measured voltage and phase at successive tap points to observe the accumulated phase shift along a matched line.

### [Lab 7A — Sinusoids, Euler, and Phasors](./07%20-%20Lab%207A%20-%20Sinusoids%20Euler%20and%20Phasors.pdf)
Used a dual-output waveform generator and oscilloscope to experimentally connect sinusoidal signals with Euler’s formula and phasor representation. Tested phase offsets, vector addition, sine/cosine components, resultant magnitude and phase, and related the concepts to quadrature modulation.

### [Lab 6 — Time Domain Reflectometry](./08%20-%20Lab%206%20-%20Time%20Domain%20Reflectometry.pdf)
Evaluated PCB and bench time-domain reflectometers by launching fast pulses into transmission lines and interpreting returned reflections. Used reflection timing and polarity to estimate cable length and distinguish matched loads, opens, shorts, and intermediate impedance discontinuities.

### [Lab 5 — Pulse Reflection and Impedance Mismatch](./09%20-%20Lab%205%20-%20Pulse%20Reflection%20and%20Impedance%20Mismatch.pdf)
Measured transmission-line reflections produced by matched and mismatched terminations using square and short-pulse excitation. Compared open-, short-, 24 Ω, 50 Ω, 75 Ω, and 500 Ω cases and explored the effect of source mismatch on reflected-voltage magnitude and polarity.

### [Labs 3–4 — Smith Chart and RF Component Analysis](./10%20-%20Labs%203-4%20-%20Smith%20Chart%20and%20RF%20Component%20Analysis.pdf)
Used a network analyzer, Smith charts, and NanoVNA test networks to characterize resistors, capacitors, and inductors across frequency. Identified self-resonant frequency and parasitic behavior, practiced SOLT calibration, verified transmission-line reflection problems with PSpice, and analyzed high-frequency component nonidealities.

### [Lab 2 — RF Cables, Connectors, and Waveguides](./11%20-%20Lab%202%20-%20RF%20Cables%20Connectors%20and%20Waveguides.pdf)
Investigated RF transmission media including coaxial cable, open-wire lines, rectangular waveguides, and common RF connectors. Measured physical dimensions, compared frequency/power constraints, and examined how geometry, dielectric properties, connector selection, and LNB frequency conversion affect high-frequency signal transmission.

### [Lab 1 — Coaxial Transmission Line Time Delay](./12%20-%20Lab%201%20-%20Coaxial%20Transmission%20Line%20Time%20Delay.pdf)
Measured time delay, phase shift, wavelength, and transfer behavior of a 50 Ω coaxial transmission line using sinusoidal and square-wave inputs. Used measured propagation delay to estimate velocity of propagation and demonstrated how transmission-line effects become increasingly important as frequency rises.

## Tools and Methods

Vector network analyzers (VNA/NanoVNA), Smith charts, S-parameters, SOL/SOLT calibration, oscilloscopes, waveform generators, coaxial transmission lines, time-domain reflectometry, slotted lines, VSWR, reflection coefficient, impedance matching, quarter-wave transformers, stub matching, RF filters, RF amplifiers, antennas, waveguides, PSpice, and microwave measurement hardware.
