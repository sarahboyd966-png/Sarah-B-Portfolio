# Industrial Electronics and PLCs - ETE 2801 Labs

Reports are ordered from the most advanced laboratory work to the earlier foundational labs, with the two in-class demonstration records placed last.

## [01 - Lab 12 - Three-Phase Induction Motors](./01%20-%20Lab%2012%20-%20Three-Phase%20Induction%20Motors.pdf)
Investigated three-phase induction motor operation using torque, speed, current, real power, reactive power, apparent power, and power-factor measurements. The lab also examined variable-frequency control, synchronous-speed relationships, stroboscopic speed verification, direction reversal by changing phase sequence, and use of the motor as a prime mover for a DC generator.

## [02 - Lab 11 - PWM Motor Control and H-Bridge](./02%20-%20Lab%2011%20-%20PWM%20Motor%20Control%20and%20H-Bridge.pdf)
Applied pulse-width modulation to practical motor control and verified the relationship between duty cycle and average voltage with a function generator and oscilloscope. The final hardware implementation used an L298N H-bridge so PWM controlled motor speed while logic inputs controlled direction.

## [03 - Lab 10 - DC Motor Load Torque Speed and Efficiency](./03%20-%20Lab%2010%20-%20DC%20Motor%20Load%20Torque%20Speed%20and%20Efficiency.pdf)
Characterized a DC motor by measuring voltage, armature current, torque, speed, input/output power, and efficiency over a range of mechanical loads. The analysis connected motor equations to measured torque-speed-current behavior and examined rated operation and stall-current concerns.

## [04 - Lab 9 - PLC Timers and Counters](./04%20-%20Lab%209%20-%20PLC%20Timers%20and%20Counters.pdf)
Implemented PLC timer and counter functions, including on-delay/off-delay timing, counter presets, accumulated values, event counting, reset logic, and physical I/O behavior using the CLICK programming environment.

## [05 - Lab 8 - PLC Motor Starter and H-Bridge](./05%20-%20Lab%208%20-%20PLC%20Motor%20Starter%20and%20H-Bridge.pdf)
Recreated a forward/reverse motor-starter control system using a PLC, physical switches, and relay outputs. The work replaced portions of hard-wired relay logic with programmed CLICK control and also analyzed a transistor-based electronic H-bridge.

## [06 - Lab 7 - Optocouplers and Electrical Isolation](./06%20-%20Lab%207%20-%20Optocouplers%20and%20Electrical%20Isolation.pdf)
Built and tested IC-style optoisolators, slotted opto-interrupters, and reflective optocouplers to study isolated signal transfer, LED drive current, phototransistor/open-collector behavior, and protection of low-voltage PLC/control electronics from higher-voltage or noisy circuitry.

## [07 - Lab 6 - Relay Motor Control and Forward-Reverse Starter](./07%20-%20Lab%206%20-%20Relay%20Motor%20Control%20and%20Forward-Reverse%20Starter.pdf)
Designed relay-based motor-control circuits while learning industrial ladder diagrams. The work progressed from basic relay logic to a motor starter and then a forward/reverse starter with normally open/closed pushbuttons, seal-in behavior, and protection against automatic restart after power restoration.

## [08 - Lab 5 - Industrial Control Devices Relays Solenoids and Contactors](./08%20-%20Lab%205%20-%20Industrial%20Control%20Devices%20Relays%20Solenoids%20and%20Contactors.pdf)
Surveyed and tested switches, indicators, electromechanical relays, solenoids, rheostats, and contactors. Manufacturer datasheets were used to compare ratings, configurations, environmental limits, mounting, lifetime, coil requirements, and application constraints.

## [09 - Lab 4 - Transformer Wiring and Voltage Regulation](./09%20-%20Lab%204%20-%20Transformer%20Wiring%20and%20Voltage%20Regulation.pdf)
Experimentally studied transformer winding identification, turns ratio, voltage/current relationships, series/parallel winding arrangements, center taps, voltage regulation, electrical isolation, and their relationship to residential 120/240 V distribution.

## [10 - Lab 3 - Three-Phase Power Wye and Delta Loads](./10%20-%20Lab%203%20-%20Three-Phase%20Power%20Wye%20and%20Delta%20Loads.pdf)
Analyzed balanced and unbalanced three-phase resistive loads in Wye and Delta configurations. Measured phase/line voltages, line currents, and neutral current were compared with phasor and impedance calculations to verify three-phase relationships.

## [11 - In-Class Demonstration - EMR vs SSR Relay Arcing](./11%20-%20In-Class%20Demonstration%20-%20EMR%20vs%20SSR%20Relay%20Arcing.pdf)
Technical response comparing electromechanical and solid-state relays, focusing on mechanical-contact arcing, wear, slower switching, electrical noise/RFI, and the absence of contact arcing in solid-state switching.

## [12 - In-Class Demonstrations - Industrial Machines and Three-Phase Waveforms](./12%20-%20In-Class%20Demonstrations%20-%20Industrial%20Machines%20and%20Three-Phase%20Waveforms.pdf)
Photographic documentation from the final laboratory session showing industrial electrical-machine and power-training hardware, including transformer/capacitor equipment, a squirrel-cage induction motor and dynamometer setup, and an oscilloscope displaying phase-shifted sinusoidal waveforms.

## Technical Scope
Three-phase Wye and Delta systems, phasor calculations, transformers, relays, contactors, solenoids, optocouplers, ladder logic, PLCs, CLICK programming, timers, counters, motor starters, H-bridges, DC motors, torque-speed characteristics, PWM, oscilloscopes, three-phase induction motors, variable-frequency drives, power factor, and industrial troubleshooting.
