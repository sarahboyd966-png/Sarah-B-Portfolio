## Modeling N₂/Ar Plasmas in somaFOAM

CAL-PLASMA Symposium · Fresno State · July 28, 2026

N₂/Ar plasma simulation work using somaFOAM, including finite-volume modeling, mesh and solver workflows, electric-field and species analysis, and investigation of plasma conditions relevant to semiconductor processes such as etching, deposition, cleaning, and surface treatment.
