# Electronic Communication Circuits and Systems - ETE 3351L

Course portfolio focused on analog and digital communication systems, frequency-domain analysis, modulation and demodulation, RF mixing, sampling, Fourier analysis, oscillators, and communication-system measurement.

## Organization

- [Projects](./Projects/) — larger course projects and demonstrations, including the radio and phase-locked-loop work.
- [Labs](./Labs/) — 14 laboratory reports arranged from the latest and most advanced work to the earlier foundational labs.

The laboratory sequence progresses from analog filtering and oscillator fundamentals through spectrum analysis, AM/FM systems, RF mixing, sampling, PLL demodulation, PWM/PAM, and advanced digital phase and quadrature modulation.
