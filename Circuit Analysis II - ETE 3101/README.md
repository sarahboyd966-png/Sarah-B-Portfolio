# Circuit Analysis II — ETE 3101

Technical reports exploring analog filter design from first-order passive networks through second-order active and specialized filter topologies. Reports are organized newest-to-oldest so the most advanced work appears first.

## Technical Reports

### [Technical Report 5 — Design and Analysis of Various Filters](./01%20-%20Technical%20Report%205.pdf)
Designed and analyzed four second-order analog filter topologies: a Sallen-Key band-pass filter, multiple-feedback band-pass filter, Butterworth unity-gain low-pass filter, and Twin-T band-stop filter. Derived transfer functions, selected component values from frequency-response and damping requirements, analyzed stability, and validated theoretical behavior with Multisim magnitude and phase simulations.

### [Technical Report 4 — Design and Analysis of Second-Order Active Filters](./02%20-%20Technical%20Report%204.pdf)
Analyzed four active band-pass filter configurations using cascaded high-pass/low-pass stages, amplification, unity-gain buffering, and an inverting topology. Derived transfer functions and examined cutoff frequency, bandwidth, quality factor, damping, poles, zeros, and stability; Multisim results and selected breadboard measurements were used to compare theoretical, simulated, and experimental behavior.

### [Technical Report 3 — Design and Analysis of Second-Order Passive Filters](./03%20-%20Technical%20Report%203.pdf)
Investigated second-order passive low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and band-stop filter behavior using RLC and RC networks. Derived transfer functions, analyzed damping and stability, selected component values, and compared calculated cutoff frequencies and −3 dB behavior with Multisim simulations and selected hardware measurements.

### [Technical Report 2 — Design and Analysis of First-Order Active Filters](./04%20-%20Technical%20Report%202.pdf)
Designed and analyzed first-order active filters using op-amps and RC networks in non-inverting and inverting low-pass and high-pass configurations. Derived active and passive transfer functions, evaluated stability and cutoff behavior, and compared theoretical results with Multisim simulations and selected breadboard measurements.

### [Technical Report 1 — First-Order Passive Filters](./05%20-%20Technical%20Report%201.pdf)
Analyzed first-order passive RC and RL low-pass and high-pass filters through Bode plots, transfer-function derivation, pole-zero analysis, and stability checks. Compared calculated cutoff and −3 dB behavior with simulated and measured results to verify the expected frequency response of each configuration.

## Tools and Methods

Multisim, breadboard prototyping, frequency-response and Bode-plot analysis, transfer functions, poles and zeros, RC/RL/RLC networks, operational amplifiers, cutoff frequency, bandwidth, quality factor, damping ratio, and stability analysis.
